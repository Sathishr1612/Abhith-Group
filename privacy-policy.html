At ABHITH MANPOWER SERVICES PVT. LTD., we respect the privacy of our clients, employees, job applicants, business partners and website visitors. We are committed to protecting the personal information entrusted to us and using it responsibly.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the nature of your interaction with us, we may collect basic information such as:

  • Name

We collect information only when it is required for legitimate business, service delivery, recruitment, communication, workforce management, verification or legal purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected may be used for:

  • Providing and managing our services

We aim to use personal information only for the purposes for which it has been collected or where its use is otherwise required for legitimate business or legal purposes.
