ABOUT ABHITH

People You Can Rely On.
Services You Can Trust.

Abhith Manpower Services Pvt. Ltd. is a professionally managed manpower, security, housekeeping and facility management solutions company committed to providing reliable, trained and customized workforce solutions to businesses, institutions, residential properties, commercial establishments and organizations.

Our services are built around four key areas: Security Solutions, Housekeeping Solutions, Facility Management and Corporate Manpower Solutions.

We provide armed and unarmed security guards, bouncers and bodyguards, drivers, corporate housekeeping personnel, building and property management support, facility management services, event support, pest control and sanitization, machine cleaning and other customized manpower solutions.

Our Corporate Manpower Solutions support organizations with dependable personnel according to their operational requirements.

We understand that every organization is different. Our services can therefore be customized according to the workplace, property, operational requirements, workforce requirements and level of service expected by the client.

Our focus goes beyond simply providing manpower. We emphasize selection, training, discipline, supervision, coordination and professional conduct.

OUR JOURNEY

From a Vision in Assam to a Growing Presence Across India

Every journey begins with a vision. For Abhith Manpower Services Pvt. Ltd., that vision has always been simple - to provide reliable manpower, professional security services, and dependable workforce solutions that businesses and organizations can trust.

  1. 2019

    Kamrup, Assam

    Our journey began in 2019 in Kamrup, Assam, with a focused commitment to delivering quality security and manpower services.

  2. Year One

    Across Assam

    Within our first year, we expanded our operations across different parts of Assam, strengthening our capabilities and building lasting relationships with clients.

  3. 2024

    Jaipur, Tripura & Uttar Pradesh

    As our experience and client partnerships grew, so did our vision. In 2024, Abhith Manpower Services expanded beyond Assam, extending its operations to Jaipur, Tripura, and Uttar Pradesh. This expansion marked an important milestone in our journey and reflected our growing ability to serve organizations across different regions of India.

  4. Today

    Towards Telangana

    Today, we are continuing our expansion towards Telangana, taking our professional manpower and workforce solutions to new markets while building a stronger regional presence.

Evolving With Our Clients' Needs

Our journey has also been defined by the evolution of our services. What began with a strong focus on manpower and security has grown into a broader range of professional workforce and facility solutions.

Today, we provide:

  • Security Solutions and Security Manpower
  • Housekeeping Services
  • Facility Management Services
  • Corporate Manpower Solutions
  • Customized Workforce Solutions

Our services are designed to support the day-to-day operational needs of businesses, institutions, and organizations while maintaining a strong focus on professionalism, reliability, discipline, and client satisfaction.
